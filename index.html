Hi, welcome to my page!

I am a fourth-year PhD student at Sant’Anna School of Advanced Studies. From June to September 2025, I will be in Frankfurt doing a PhD internship in the Fiscal Policies Division at the European Central Bank (ECB).

During April-September 2024 I visited the School of Economics and Finance of Queen Mary University of London.

My main research interests lie in the field of Applied Macroeconomics, primarily in three research areas:

  • The macroeconomic effects of aggregate demand dynamics in the medium- and
    long-run (hysteresis effects), placing particular attention on nonlinear
    transmission mechanisms.
  • The macroeconomic impacts of climate change, particularly how climate-induced
    shocks affect tail risks in GDP growth.
  • Evaluation of the empirical performance of macroeconomic models using time-
    series techniques, with a focus on both Agent-based and DSGE models .
